You work the same hours, but each day feels more exhausting than ever before. You're bouncing between problems constantly, and every switch costs energy. Burnout seems to happen faster in the codegen era.

I've found it helpful to intentionally refine my workflow and where my attention goes. When you get it right, the pacing can be an incredible productivity boost. Pick up a problem, know when to pause it, and be willing to leave an agent half-finished for a couple of days if you're not feeling it. Get the pacing right and you get the dopamine.

Finding the right workflow for you takes time and intention, but it's hugely beneficial long-term.
