Rüdersdorf

August 29, 2024 · Rüdersdorf bei Berlin, Brandenburg, Germany

Approximate area only. Exact locations aren't shared.

I always called this the concrete plant, and I was half right. It was built from 1940 to 1942 as a “super cement works,” supposedly the most modern in Germany, right next to the giant Rüdersdorf limestone pit that's supplied Berlin's stone and lime for centuries. After the war the Soviets took the machinery as reparations, and from 1950 the GDR used it to make phosphate fertilizer and animal feed. It closed in 1999.

Now it's rows of concrete silos, a pair of huge chimneys, kilns, conveyors, collapsing roofs and a lot of graffiti. It's also a popular film set: The Hunger Games: Mockingjay Part 2 shot District 8 here, along with The Monuments Men and Enemy at the Gates. It's fenced off, full of asbestos, and officially very much not open to the public. I've been twice.
