Fürstenberg

June 24, 2026 · Fürstenberg/Havel, Brandenburg, Germany

Approximate area only. Exact locations aren't shared.

This giant ruin sits right on the edge of the Röblinsee in Fürstenberg. It was built in 1912 as the Behrn-Mühle, a flour mill that was supposedly one of the biggest in northern Germany. In GDR times it became a state-run animal feed plant, and the whole area around the lake was taken over by the Soviet army from 1945 until 1993. Now it's just a huge empty shell, slowly falling in on itself.

The whole town clearly doesn't love having an abandoned flour mill in the middle of it, but they kind of tolerate it. There are fences up that say “do not enter,” and there's graffiti everywhere anyway. There were kids hanging out inside, and people kayaking on the river saw us and waved. Evidently it's a place people come to see.

That said, parts of it had clearly been secured. Someone had made it harder to climb up high or get into the more dangerous spots. A little bit of childproofing.

We climbed all the way to the top. It was one of the most dangerous things I've ever done in urbex, and the view was worth it.

This building is rickety and terrifying. It's a wooden structure, and a lot of it is rotting. There were so many pieces I could have fallen straight through, and the floors are so far gone you can look down through every level to the ground. I don't know how much life it has left in it.
