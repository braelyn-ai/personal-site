Ferry Point

December 7, 2025 · Richmond, California

Approximate area only. Exact locations aren't shared.

Ferry Point in Richmond was the very end of the Santa Fe Railway starting in 1900. Trains couldn't cross the bay, so they'd roll the railcars straight onto ferries here and float them to San Francisco. That kept going into the 1960s. What's left is a rusty gantry over the water and an old terminal building that's now completely covered in graffiti.
