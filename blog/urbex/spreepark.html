Spreepark

August 6, 2025 demolished · Plänterwald, Berlin, Germany

Approximate area only. Exact locations aren't shared.

Round 1

The first time I went to Spreepark, we made it in for about five minutes. We were loud, drunk, and taking flash photos, so it's not a huge surprise that security nabbed us within ten. They escorted us out and told us, very politely, “Please do not come back.”

Round 2

Naturally, the next year I had to go back. This time I came with a plan, and I got far more photos. By then, though, most of the park had been demolished, and I'd missed a lot of the coolest shots you can find online.
